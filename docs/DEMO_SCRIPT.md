# Two-Minute Demo Script

## First minute

- **0–10s:** Show the homepage disclosure and citizen problem.
- **10–20s:** Sign in with `9999999999` / `123456` and show the dashboard.
- **20–35s:** Resume Learner Licence; demonstrate prefill, autosave, and the document assistant.
- **35–45s:** Add three demo documents and choose the recommended appointment.
- **45–55s:** Review and complete the clearly labelled test payment.
- **55–60s:** Show the application number, tracking timeline, and next action.

## Second minute

- Explain guided steps, local persistence, accessibility, responsive design, and multilingual preference.
- Show synthetic vehicle/challan records and the help centre.
- Clarify that identity, government records, appointments, payments, OCR, and processing are simulated.
- Close with the authorized-integration and DPDP-compliant production pathway.
